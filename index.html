Questions.

What do I need on my server?

ModelEngine and MythicMobs are required. MCPets is needed for companions. ItemsAdder is optional (3D head icons for menus and shops).

Does it work for Bedrock players?

Yes. Every creature comes with the Geyser files, so Bedrock players see the same models and animations.

How do I get the files?

Add what you want to the cart, then send your order to Banque on Discord. Once it's paid, you receive a download with one folder per plugin. Copy them into plugins/ and reload.

Can I edit or resell them?

You can use them on your own server(s) and tweak the configs. Reselling or sharing the files is not allowed.

Why are the names in French?

They were made for a French server. You can rename them in the MCPets and MythicMobs configs.
